Stature: Height Growth Tracker · iOS and Android

Need help with Stature? Email ismail.karakasmaras@gmail.com — we usually reply within 2 business days.

1Subscriptions

Restore a purchase: open the Stature Pro screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID or Google account you purchased with.

Cancel or manage: on iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stature. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds: payments are processed by Apple or Google. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com or through Google Play.

2Your data

Stature has no account. Your growth profile and measurements are stored only on your device.

  • Export: Profile → Export measurements.
  • Delete: Profile → Delete all data, or uninstall the app.

See the Privacy Policy for details.

3About the estimates

Stature compares your height with the CDC stature-for-age reference and shows statistical estimates. It is not medical advice. Talk to a doctor about any concerns regarding growth or development.

4Contact

Email ismail.karakasmaras@gmail.com. Please include your device model and app version (Profile → bottom of the screen).
