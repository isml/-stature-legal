Privacy Policy

Effective September 16, 2026 · Applies to the Stature: Height Growth Tracker app (app.stature.imk)

The short version: Stature doesn't require an account, your growth profile (height, age, family reference, daily habits) is stored only on your device, we don't sell your data, and we don't show ads. This page explains exactly what the app does send off your device, and why.

1Overview

Stature ("we", "us", "the app") is a height-tracking and growth-estimate app published by İsmail Karakaş. This policy describes what information the app collects, how it's used, and who it's shared with. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of Stature.

2No account required

Stature does not ask you to sign up, and does not collect your name, email address, or phone number to use the app. On first launch, the app generates a random device identifier and an anonymous authentication token (see Third parties) purely to keep your local data stable between sessions and to correlate crash reports — neither is tied to your real-world identity.

3What we collect

CategoryExamplesWhere it lives
Growth profile Age, sex, current height, family height reference, sleep / activity / nutrition answers, measurement history On your device only. Never uploaded to a Stature server — we don't operate one.
Usage analytics Screens viewed, features used, app-open events — no profile values attached Firebase Analytics (Google), tied to an anonymous device ID
Crash & performance data Stack traces, device model, OS version Firebase Crashlytics (Google)
Purchase data Subscription status, plan, purchase token RevenueCat and the Google Play / App Store billing system

We do not collect photos, contacts, location, or precise identity information at any point.

4How we use this information

  • To calculate your on-device height estimate, growth velocity, and routine recommendations.
  • To understand which features are actually used, so we can improve the app.
  • To diagnose and fix crashes.
  • To unlock Stature Pro features after a verified purchase, and to let you restore a purchase on a new device.

5Third-party services we use

We use a small number of well-known infrastructure providers. We don't sell or rent your information to anyone, and we don't use it for third-party advertising.

Firebase (Google) — Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config, and anonymous authentication. Google Privacy Policy ↗

RevenueCat — manages and validates Stature Pro subscriptions across stores. RevenueCat Privacy Policy ↗

Google Play / Apple App Store billing — processes your actual payment. We never see or store your card details.

6Storage & retention

Your growth profile and measurement history live in local app storage on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes this data permanently — we have no copy to restore it from. Anonymous analytics and crash events are retained by Firebase per Google's standard retention windows (typically up to 14 months for Analytics).

7Your choices

  • Delete your data: open Profile → Delete all data in the app, or uninstall the app — either removes your entire local growth profile.
  • Export your data: Profile → Export measurements saves your measurement history as a CSV file.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription: through Google Play → Subscriptions, or the App Store → Subscriptions on iOS.
  • Opt out of analytics identifiers: contact us (below) and we'll help you request deletion from Firebase.

8Children's privacy

Stature is intended for a general and teen audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data through analytics identifiers, contact us and we will delete it.

9Not medical advice

Height and growth estimates in Stature are statistical projections for informational purposes only. They are not a medical diagnosis and don't replace advice from a pediatrician or healthcare provider — especially if you have concerns about growth, puberty, or development.

10Security

We rely on your device's built-in app sandboxing to protect locally stored data, and on Firebase's and RevenueCat's own security practices for the limited data sent to them. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but nothing here is more sensitive than typical fitness-app data.

11Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the effective date above and, for material changes, surface a notice inside the app.
